# Replace the hydraulic return filter on the GX-40 press

This procedure is for the hydraulic return filter on the GX-40 press.

## Stop the press and let the oil become cool

1. Stop the press.
2. Isolate the press with a lockout device.
3. Let the oil become cool for approximately 10 minutes.

## Remove the old filter element

4. Put a drip tray under the filter housing.

**WARNING:** DO NOT DRAIN THE OIL WHEN IT IS HOT. THE OIL CAN BE 70 °C OR HOTTER AND CAN CAUSE BURNS.

5. Remove the drain plug.
6. Let the oil drain into the drip tray.
7. Remove the four housing bolts.
8. Remove the old filter element from the filter housing.
9. Put the old filter element and the old O-ring in the oily waste bin.

## Clean and examine the filter housing

10. Clean the inside of the filter housing with a lint-free cloth.
11. Examine the seat of the filter housing for scoring.
12. If the seat has scoring, do these steps:
    a. Stop the procedure.
    b. Tell the maintenance supervisor to replace the filter housing.
13. If the seat has no scoring, continue with the next step.

## Install the new filter element

14. Get the new filter element, part number HF-2210.
15. Put the new filter element in the filter housing.
16. Apply a small amount of clean hydraulic oil to the new O-ring.

NOTE: Without oil, the O-ring can become damaged when you install it.

17. Install the new O-ring.
18. Install the filter housing.
19. Install the four housing bolts.

**CAUTION:** DO NOT TIGHTEN THE BOLTS TOO MUCH. THE FILTER HOUSING IS MADE OF ALUMINUM. THIS CAN CAUSE DAMAGE TO THE THREADS.

20. Tighten the four housing bolts to 35 Nm in a cross pattern.

## Fill the reservoir and examine for leaks

21. Install the drain plug.
22. Add approximately 4.5 L of ISO VG 46 hydraulic oil to the reservoir.
23. Remove the lockout device.
24. Operate the press at idle for 5 minutes.

NOTE: The filter indicator on the HMI resets automatically after approximately 1 minute of operation. No action is necessary.

25. Examine the area around the filter housing for leaks.
