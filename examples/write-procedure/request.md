Notes - hydraulic return filter change on the GX-40 press:
- machine should be shut down and locked out first obviously, then you've got to wait about 10 min so the oil cools down (it runs at 70 C+, guys have been burned before when draining)
- put a drip tray under the filter housing, unscrew the drain plug & let it drain, then the 4 housing bolts come off and you can pull the old element out. old element + old o-ring go in the oily waste bin
- wipe the inside of the housing w/ a lint free cloth, check the seat for scoring. if there's scoring housing needs replacing, stop and call maintenance supervisor
- new element is part no. HF-2210, new o-ring should be lubed with a bit of clean hydraulic oil before fitting otherwise it can pinch
- refit housing, bolts need to be torqued to 35 Nm in a cross pattern. don't overtighten - housing is aluminium and threads strip easily
- drain plug back in, top up reservoir with approx 4.5 L of ISO VG 46, remove lockout, run the press at idle for 5 minutes and check for leaks around the housing
- fyi the filter indicator on the HMI resets by itself after about 1 min of running, you don't need to do anything
