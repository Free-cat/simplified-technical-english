# HP-200 Hydraulic Power Unit: Operator Guide (Section 4)

## 4.1 General

The HP-200 is a mobile hydraulic power unit. It supplies hydraulic pressure to aircraft systems during ground maintenance when the aircraft engines do not operate. The HP-200 has these components:

- a 15 kW electric motor
- a variable-displacement pump
- a 60 L reservoir
- a control panel

Use the pressure selector to adjust the output pressure between 500 and 3000 psi. You must keep the reservoir at least three-quarters full at all times. If the pump operates with too little fluid, cavitation occurs. Cavitation can cause damage to the pump.

## 4.2 Start the unit

1. Do a check of the fluid level in the sight glass.

WARNING: Do not use your hand to find leaks. Hydraulic fluid under pressure can penetrate the skin and cause serious injury.

2. Examine the unit for leaks.
3. Wear eye protection.
4. Connect the pressure hose and the return hose to the ground service panel of the aircraft.
5. Make sure that the quick-disconnect couplings are fully engaged.
6. Open the shut-off valve.
7. Make sure that the pressure selector is at minimum.
8. Push the green START button.
9. Slowly turn the pressure selector clockwise until the gauge shows the necessary pressure.
10. Monitor the unit while it operates.
11. If the red OVERTEMP light illuminates, stop the unit immediately.

## 4.3 Stop the unit

1. Decrease the pressure to minimum.
2. Push the STOP button.
3. Close the shut-off valve.
4. Let the residual pressure decrease for approximately 2 minutes.
5. Wear eye protection.
6. Disconnect the hoses.
