# HP-200 Hydraulic Power Unit: Operator Guide (Section 4)

## 4.1 General

The HP-200 is a mobile hydraulic power unit which is utilised for supplying hydraulic pressure to aircraft systems during ground maintenance when the aircraft engines aren't running. It comprises a 15 kW electric motor, a variable-displacement pump, a 60 L reservoir and a control panel; the unit's output pressure can be adjusted between 500 and 3000 psi by means of the pressure selector. The reservoir should be kept at least three-quarters full at all times, since running the pump with insufficient fluid will result in cavitation and the pump may be damaged.

## 4.2 Starting the unit

1. Before starting, the fluid level should be checked in the sight glass and the unit should be inspected for leaks.
2. Connect the pressure and return hoses to the aircraft ground service panel, ensuring that the quick-disconnect couplings are fully engaged, then open the shut-off valve.
3. Having confirmed that the pressure selector is at minimum, press the green START button.
4. Slowly rotate the pressure selector clockwise until the required pressure is indicated on the gauge.
5. The unit must be monitored while it's operating. If the red OVERTEMP light illuminates, shut down the unit immediately.

Note: Always wear eye protection when connecting or disconnecting hoses.

Hydraulic fluid under pressure can penetrate the skin and can cause serious injury, so you should never check for leaks with your hand. WARNING!

## 4.3 Shutting down

Reduce pressure to minimum, press STOP, close the shut-off valve and wait approx. 2 minutes for residual pressure to bleed off prior to disconnecting hoses.
